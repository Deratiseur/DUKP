Introduction


This mod began as a kit pack intended to make rarely played single classes more appealing by giving them interesting kits. Hence the name “Unused”.

Over time, more kits were added, and some, such as the Warlord and the Bladesinger, moved away from that original purpose. DUKP remains a collection of carefully crafted kits into which I have put all my know-how, hoping that you will find something you enjoy. Have fun :)

Hatred CarrierHalf-orc fighter

On the Sword Coast, near the Ten Towns, old wars against combined armies of orcs and goblins have left the inhabitants with a deep hatred of those peoples. Half-orcs born to human women raped by orcs are usually killed at birth. Some mothers, however, cannot bring themselves to abandon their children, whatever their ancestry. These children are raised in secret, for the townsfolk would show no mercy even to a child with orc blood. Hidden in dark cellars or foul attics, always afraid of discovery and death, they know only a mother who teaches them that the world hates them. Hatred carriers grow up around a single thought: the world hates me, and I hate it in return. They become ruthless brutes who know only strength, resentment, and anger. Their half-orc heritage gives them exceptional physical strength, but their unstable minds severely hamper their ability to reason or learn. They know only "brutal" abilities and show no finesse.

Advantages:

  • Gains one daily use of Intimidation every 4 levels. This works like Charm Person, but lasts longer and imposes harsher saving throw penalties. It grows stronger with level. The victim is always hostile when the effect ends.
  • At level 7 and every 5 levels thereafter, gains one daily use of Roar of Rage. Like Horror, it forces nearby creatures to save or flee in fear for 45 seconds. Its power grows with level.
  • At level 12 and every 6 levels thereafter, gains one daily use of Fist of Fury. For 2 rounds, gains an additional attack and deals maximum damage with every attack. Each hit also has a chance to stun the victim.
  • Indomitable: Immune to charm.
  • Brutal: +1 to damage.

Disadvantages:

  • Must be chaotic neutral, neutral evil, or chaotic evil.
  • Receives 10% less experience because of his limited ability to learn.
  • Begins with -1 to Intelligence, Charisma, and Wisdom because of his lack of education.
Note for this kit

When playing a Hatred Carrier, you can skip the War Cry high-level ability. Roar of Rage produces the same effect, with an additional saving throw penalty that increases with level, but a shorter duration. This makes War Cry redundant.

Vermin HunterRanger

Even the best-protected human cities are never entirely safe from pests such as rats and molds. In woodland settlements built among the trees, giant spiders, beetles, and other insects may nest nearby and disturb the peace. Vermin hunters are trained to drive out and exterminate these creatures. Years of practice give them a natural resistance to toxins and the ability to treat poisoning in others. They can also move freely through webs, entanglements, and other obstacles that restrict movement. Among elves, these rangers serve their community; among humans, they are usually hired and paid as civic guards.

Advantages:

  • +3 to hit and damage against ankhegs, otyughs, slimes, and giant vermin.
  • At level 4, gains freedom of movement: immunity to webs, entanglement, immobilization, and slowing effects.
  • At level 8, becomes immune to poison.
  • At level 10, becomes immune to unnatural diseases.
  • At level 4 and every 4 levels thereafter, may use Slow Poison once per day.
  • At level 7 and every 5 levels thereafter, may neutralize toxins once per day.
  • At level 12 and every 6 levels thereafter, may use Vermin Summoning once per day.
  • At level 15 and every 8 levels thereafter, may use Giant Vermin Summoning once per day.

Disadvantages:

  • May not wear armor heavier than studded leather.
  • May not learn proficiencies in two-handed weapons other than bows and quarterstaves.

VERMIN SUMMONING:

An experienced vermin hunter instinctively knows where pests lurk nearby and can use his ranger skills to tame them. Each use calls up to 4 creatures that join him for 2 minutes.

GIANT VERMIN SUMMONING:

After years of fighting vermin, the hunter can train and summon even the most feared creatures. Up to 3 creatures answer his call and join him for 2 minutes.

Notes for this kit

Flame PreacherCleric

Fire has long inspired admiration among humanoids. Pyromaniacs kindle it for the sheer pleasure of watching it consume everything. Wizards value its destructive power. Tacticians use it to frighten, repel and corner their enemies. Some clerics also become fascinated by this force of nature, and the less prudent may even deem it worthy of worship. By rejecting their original deity, they lose their ability to cast divine spells. Among these fallen priests, those most fanatically devoted to fire and destruction sometimes attract the attention of dark powers, usually demonic in origin. These powers restore their spellcasting, but corrupt it with fire and strip it of all restorative abilities. They turn the fallen clerics into instruments of devastation, without necessarily revealing their intentions.

Advantages:

  • Gains additional fire spells:Level 1: Flame Dart, Scorcher, Burning Hands.Level 2: Magma Bomb, Explosion, Rain of Fire, Lower Fire Resistance, Fire Seeds.Level 3: Blazing Weapon, Fire Shield (Red), Fireball, Flame Arrow, Melf's Minute Meteors.Level 4: Conjure Lesser Fire Elemental, Ignition, Fire Form, Lower Fire Resistance II, Fire Breath.Level 5: Call Fire Servant, Ardent Mantle, Fire Mark, Mass Protection from Fire, Pulverization.Level 6: Heat Aura, Elemental Fireball, Conjure Fire Elemental, Detonation, Exploding Mephits.Level 7: Incineration, Fire Trap.
  • Gains 10% fire resistance at level 1, plus 3% per subsequent level.
  • May use Burning Palm once per day at level 10, gaining another daily use every 3 levels thereafter.
  • May charm a fire elemental once per day at level 13, gaining another daily use at level 20.

Disadvantages:

  • Cannot cast ANY healing, curing or restoration spells.
  • Cannot turn undead.
  • Must be true neutral or neutral evil.
  • Cannot dual-class: his obsession with fire rules out any other career.
  • Does not receive a divine symbol at level 25, having severed all ties to the gods.

BURNING PALM:

After activating this ability, the preacher has two rounds to touch an opponent. A successful touch deals 1d6 points of fire damage per two preacher levels. If he fails to hit within that time, the spell is lost.

CHARM FIRE ELEMENTAL:

The preacher can attempt to control any fire elemental by touching it. The target may save with a -1 penalty per 5 preacher levels to resist. If it fails, it serves the preacher for 5 turns.

Recall of the Fire Army

If you are lucky enough to resurrect a pyromancer mage, remember that after casting his strongest area and summoning spells and releasing his sequencers or contingencies, he still has plenty of spells that you can order him to cast, choosing the targets yourself. Crusader of GaragosCleric

Legends say that Garagos was the god of war before Tempus killed him and stole his portfolio in a titanic battle. But Garagos is certainly not dead. He lives on and champions the violent aspects of combat: carnage, destruction and pillaging. For Garagos, negotiation and tactics are the acts of the weak. Ambushes and retreats are the acts of cowards. Only man-to-man confrontation is honorable. Crusaders of Garagos thus strive to wreak destruction wherever they go. Few villages remain standing after they have passed through, and few survivors can tell of an encounter with these crusaders.

Advantages:

  • May specialize in any weapon available to clerics.
  • May invest 3 proficiency points in Two-Weapon Style.
  • His THAC0 progression broadly follows that of a fighter: +1 every 3 levels from level 2.
  • He is immune to charms.
  • May enter a rage once a day for 1 turn. Enraged, he gains +2 to hit, damage and saving throws, +1 to initiative, and always attacks the nearest creature, even if it is an ally.
  • At 7th level and every 5 levels thereafter he can use "Whirlwind Attack" once a day.
  • At level 12 and every 7 levels thereafter he can use "Blade Barrier" once a day.
  • At level 15 he gains one extra attack per round when he goes into a rage.
  • At level 20 he automatically casts "Bless" and "Chant" when he goes into a rage.
  • He can choose fighter high-level abilities as well as priest high-level abilities.

Disadvantages:

  • He cannot turn undead.
  • May cast one fewer spell per spell level.
  • He does not receive a divine symbol at high level.
  • He can only be lawful evil or chaotic evil.

WHIRLWIND ATTACK:

This ability allows the crusader to unleash a flurry of lightning-fast blows. For its duration, the crusader makes 10 attacks per round but suffers a -4 penalty to attack and damage rolls. The Whirlwind Attack lasts for 6 seconds.

BLADE BARRIER:

This ability creates a wall of circling, razor-sharp blades. These whirl and flash around the crusader, creating an impenetrable barrier. Any creature attempting to pass through the Blade Barrier suffers 8d8 points of damage (Saving Throw vs. Spell to negate the damage). The barrier remains for 1 turn.

Shadow WalkerThief

The order of shadow walkers is an ancient organization whose members share a special affinity with shadow. Rumor has it that its greatest masters are living shadows shaped by magic. Whatever the truth, this bond gives the order a valuable advantage. Only the masters may appoint new recruits. An initiate who accepts their offer is assigned a mentor to teach him shadow techniques and the magic associated with them. Every full member can use shadows to his advantage and wield simple spells. True shadow magic, however, is practiced only by the oldest masters. Shadow Walkers are not ordinary thieves. Dedicated to good, they swear to use their extraordinary abilities to avenge victims. Acting as vigilantes, they hunt down criminals who have escaped conventional justice.

Advantages:

  • Uses the normal thief THAC0 progression, with an additional cumulative -1 bonus at levels 5, 11, 17, 23, and 29.
  • May study all magical scrolls from the schools of Abjuration, Alteration, Illusion, and Divination, then use shadow magic to cast those spells as magical abilities, up to 4 times per day per spell level. Gains access to a higher spell level every 4 levels, up to spell level 4.
  • Gains +1 to saving throws vs. wands and spells.
  • Can see in the dark as though using infravision.
  • At levels 3, 5, and 7, gains one daily use of Aura of Shadows.
  • At levels 8, 10, and 12, gains one daily use of Mantle of Shadows.
  • At levels 13, 15, and 17, gains one daily use of Shape of Shadow.

Disadvantages:

  • Only humans, elves, and half-elves may be chosen.
  • Must be of good alignment.
  • Cannot use longswords.
  • Gains only 20 points to distribute among thief skills at each level.

AURA OF SHADOWS:

A partly magical aura surrounds the shadow walker, granting +25% to Hide in Shadows for 1 round per level.

MANTLE OF SHADOWS:

The shadow walker becomes partly translucent, gaining a +1 concealment bonus to AC and saving throws, and +50% to Hide in Shadows. Remaining motionless for at least 1 round makes him invisible. He can repeat this throughout the effect's duration of 1 round per level.

SHAPE OF SHADOW:

The shadow walker becomes a living shadow. He is almost invisible and gains +4 to AC and saving throws. He may attack or cast spells without becoming visible, although creatures may occasionally glimpse him as a movement in the shadows. Detect Invisibility cannot reveal him, while True Seeing reveals him only intermittently. Only magical weapons can harm him. His weapons become ethereal and pass harmlessly through targets, so he cannot use them. Instead, his touch deals 1D4+1 damage and drains 1 point of Strength for 2 turns if the victim fails a saving throw vs. death at -3. He may remain in this form for 1 round per level or return to normal at will.

Shadow MageGnome thief

Visitors from other planes sometimes leave subtle traces on Toril. Gnomes are no strangers to such encounters, and their descendants may bear unexpected signs of that heritage generations later. A gnome thief learning to blend into the shadows may begin to feel that he is truly becoming part of them. He may fear this sensation, or see it as a chance to transcend his nature. A shadowmage is such a thief, with a newly discovered ancestry linked to shadow. Drawing on this unexpected gift and his natural affinity for illusions, he learns to shape the dark energy of the demiplane into powers resembling spells.

Advantages:

  • Casts shadow and illusion spells like a sorcerer, up to 4 spells per spell level each day. Gains access to a higher spell level every 3 thief levels.
  • Gains +2% to Hide in Shadows and +3% to Move Silently each level.
  • As his mastery grows, he infuses more shadow into his illusions and can cast spells using shadow matter at lower spell levels than a mage.

Disadvantages:

  • Can only cast shadow and illusion spells.
  • Cannot use items restricted to mages.
  • Shaping the demiplane's energy is dangerous. Each spell has a 10% chance, minus 1% per level, of draining an experience level or a point of Constitution. The risk disappears at level 11. Lost Constitution returns after a night's sleep; lost experience levels require an appropriate restorative spell.
  • Developing his powers slows his training as a thief: gains only 15 points to distribute among thief skills each level and receives 20% less experience.

High-level abilities

The Shadow Mage does not gain particularly powerful spells through high-level abilities: its spell progression already extends to level 24. Instead, it offers six successive stages that grant considerable power by transforming a gnome into a partly corporeal reflection. Completing these stages before taking standard thief abilities is strongly recommended: the experience penalty prevents the character from acquiring every available ability.

A strategy bordering on cheating

Casting Project Image makes the Shadow Mage invisible. This invisibility lasts as long as the image exists and allows a backstab with every attack. Simply leave the image somewhere safe, such as an already explored part of the map, to remain invisible for the spell’s full duration.

Savant ArtillerymanMage

See the notes and progression tables

Magic Missile is an iconic example of raw arcane power and a favorite of young mages. It is hardly surprising that some devote their studies to this spell above all others. The Savant Artilleryman is one of them, fascinated by its unerring accuracy.

Obsessively studying every rune and magical symbol that makes up the spell, he learns to break its limits and twist its energy into new effects. With every facet of the spell etched into his mind, he becomes a true expert, able to turn its dweomer into an instrument of devastation.

Through relentless use of this spell, he abandons the principles of his school and earns a new title: Savant Artilleryman.

Advantages:

  • At level 1 he learns a special version of the "Magic Missile" spell, which cannot be prevented by dead magic, miscasting, etc.
  • At level 5 he can cast "Shield" as a special ability once a day.
  • At level 7 he automatically learns a special version of "Mordenkainen's Force Missiles" which cannot be blocked by the "Shield" spell.
  • At level 9 he breaks the Magic Missile spell limit: +1 missile every three levels, up to a maximum of 11 missiles at level 27.
  • At level 11, his Shield ability is upgraded to block magical projectiles from wands, traps, special abilities, and other sources.
  • At level 13 he improves the spell "Mordenkainen's Force Missiles" so that it casts faster (-1 to casting time) and imposes -2 to the saving throw against the explosion.
  • At level 15 he improves the Magic Missile spell so that each missile has a 10% chance of piercing the target's magic resistance.
  • At level 17 his Shield ability is upgraded to send back any magic missiles he is the target of.
  • At level 19 he further improves the "Mordenkainen's Force Missiles" spell: the area of impact of the explosion is increased by 15% and if the saving throw fails the targets affected remain stunned for 1D2 rounds.
  • At level 21 and every 5 levels thereafter, he gains a free use of Magic Missile as a special ability once a day.
  • At level 23 he further improves the Magic Missile spell so that each missile has a 10% chance of inflicting a critical hit (double damage).
  • At level 25 he breaks the limit of Mordenkainen's Force Missiles spell: +1 missile every 3 subsequent levels until the 11th missile at level 37.

Disadvantages:

  • He has the same restrictions as an invoker: the Enchantment school is forbidden.
  • Does not receive the additional memorization slots normally granted to specialist mages.
  • He does not use scrolls or wands of Magic Missile. They would be wasted: he cannot reshape spells already stored in such items.

Exalted Fighter: before dual-classing

Some warriors live only to fight, swear by war, and dream of nothing but victory. The most fanatical seek every possible source of power to crush their foes. If such a fighter discovers an innate gift for magic, he may see it as another source of strength and choose to learn how to wield it. In doing so, he becomes a warlord.

Advantages:

  • No special advantages until he becomes a mage.
  • To dual-class into a mage, he needs only 17 Intelligence and 12 Wisdom; his physical ability scores do not matter.

Disadvantages:

  • Considers only true weapons of war worthy of study.
  • His obsession with power prevents him from being lawful good.
  • Regards ranged combat as cowardly and refuses to use missile weapons.

A warlord is a fanatical fighter who sees his innate arcane gift as a way to become a merciless engine of war. While continuing his martial training, he focuses his magical discoveries on what he considers essential: becoming stronger in battle and devastating enemy ranks. Ignoring traditional magic, he develops his own repertoire of spells, all intended for combat. A proud warrior, he shuns "cowardly" tactics such as sheltering behind summoned creatures or fighting from a distance. He has no interest in honorable combat, however, and will inflict the worst torments on his foes, from mental ruin to utter annihilation.

Advantages:

  • Because magic comes to him instinctively, he can continue his martial training, retaining some of his fighting abilities:
  • Gains 1 additional hit point per level.
  • Gains a +1 bonus to his saving throw vs. death every 4 levels, up to +5.
  • Uses the cleric THAC0 progression.
  • May cast spells in any armor once he has regained his fighter abilities.
  • Reflection during moments of respite allows him to discover new spells regularly.
  • His spell memorization capacity grows faster than that of a conventional mage.

Disadvantages:

  • His magic is innate; he does not understand arcane theory and can neither use magical scrolls nor learn spells from them.
  • Time spent on martial training slows his progress as a mage (-15% experience).
  • Regards ranged combat as cowardly and refuses to use missile weapons.
Notes for this kit

The idea behind the kit

This kit grew from the desire to create a character who uses magic without following the traditional divine or arcane paths. The Warlord wears armor and develops his own repertoire of combat spells. The original idea was that he would find his strength within himself and bend magic to his will; the engine’s limitations led to a fighter dual-classed to mage.

Creating a Warlord

Create a human Exalted Fighter, then dual-class to mage: the character automatically becomes a Warlord.

Two paths for high-level abilities

The arcane research path:

  • Allows the use of mage scrolls.
  • Improves certain spells and removes their drawbacks.
  • Grants a contingency spell and a spell-combination ability.
  • Then allows beneficial spells to be cast on any party member, and eventually on the entire party.
  • Optionally allows the development of level “10” spells.
  • Once sufficiently developed, level 10 spells grant a few additional level 9 spell slots.

The inner research path:

  • Grants immunity to silence, wild magic, and miscast magic.
  • Halves spell casting times.
  • Eventually allows any spell to be cast at any time, like a sorcerer.

Gaining several levels that grant high-level abilities at once can result in duplicate or even triplicate spells. You can remove the duplicates with ShadowKeeper or EEKeeper. This problem does not occur when levels are gained one at a time.

AnatomistThief / necromancer
Character creation: select the thief class.

An anatomist is a thief versed in the dark arts who studies corpses in search of ways to perfect the backstabbing techniques of his profession. Through painstaking study of the body's hidden structures and repeated practice on weak reanimated creatures, the necromancer uncovers secrets of the flesh that go beyond simply striking vital organs. He becomes an anatomist, so familiar with living bodies that he always knows where to strike for maximum harm, without needing to hide.

Advantages:

  • +1 to critical hit chance, increasing to +2 at level 5, +3 at level 11, +4 at level 18, and +5 at level 26.
  • +1 to THAC0 every 4 levels.

Disadvantages:

  • Cannot backstab.
  • Does not receive bonus spells for specializing in necromancy.
Special: After character creation, the anatomist automatically becomes a multiclass thief/necromancer.
Notes for this kit

Create an Anatomist by selecting a thief. After the game begins, the character automatically becomes a thief/necromancer multiclass and receives a few spells, some chosen at random.

Although the description does not mention it, the Anatomist can gain +6 to critical hit chance at level 35. This level is normally unreachable in the unmodified game: it requires 11 million experience points, whereas the cap is 8 million. To benefit from this bonus, remove the experience cap and add enough quest or combat mods to earn a substantial amount of XP. I implemented the bonus anyway, for continuity and for power gamers who enjoy being powerful ^^.

BladesingerElf fighter / mage
Character creation: select the fighter class.

Few elven warriors are as dangerous as bladesingers. They spend their entire lives studying their chosen weapon until they become its absolute masters. They also learn to cast spells in the midst of battle, making them doubly formidable.

A bladesinger's sure-footed movements are more than a display of grace: they place him where he can fight most effectively. He instinctively follows the course of battle and chooses the right maneuvers to make each strike count. He can also draw upon the flow of magic around him. Although he cannot attack while casting a spell, this magic lets him defend himself against melee attacks.

An unwavering defender of the elven faith, a bladesinger must further the elven cause at all times and aid any elf in need. Unless that elf is proven to be an enemy of the elven faith, he must do everything he can to save them, even at the cost of his own life.

Advantages:

  • May achieve grand mastery in one-handed long blades.
  • May cast spells while wearing armor.
  • +1 to speed factor, with an additional +1 every 4 levels.
  • Gains an AC bonus equal to half his fighter level + 1. This bonus does not apply against missiles.
  • At level 10, gains +1 to hit and damage.

Disadvantages:

  • May only use one-handed long blades.
  • May not use missile weapons.
  • May not wear armor heavier than studded leather.
  • May not learn Two-Weapon Style or Two-Handed Weapon Style.
Special: After character creation, the bladesinger automatically becomes a multiclass fighter/mage.
Notes for this kit

Create a Bladesinger by selecting an elf fighter. After the game begins, the character automatically becomes a fighter/mage multiclass and receives a few spells, some chosen at random.

Installing the kit also adds a special suit of armor in BGT (the BG1 portion), BGEE, and IWDEE. It appears late in the adventure and only if the party includes a Bladesinger.

Theurgical ExplorerHuman cleric / wild mage
Character creation: select the cleric class.

A theurgical explorer is a devoted priest of Azuth who has made it his sacred mission to add as many spells as possible to the god's great encyclopedia. To do so, he embraces arcane magic and explores one of its most unusual branches: wild magic. Others in the faith have done the same, but his profound passion for magic sets him apart in his god's eyes. Only when he vows to dedicate himself entirely to exploring magic in all its forms may he be granted a complete transformation of his divine abilities to help him carry out that mission. He then ceases to be an ordinary priest and becomes a theurgical explorer.

Advantages:

  • Divine dweomer protection: His spells cannot fail through miscast magic or dead magic, and armor does not prevent him from casting mage spells. He can still be interrupted, paralyzed, or silenced.
  • Divine advantage: At levels 8 and 15, gains a -1 bonus to spell casting time.
  • Special divine spell: May cast a special version of Wondrous Recall that restores two priest spells AND two mage spells.
  • Divine reward: Receives the holy symbol of Azuth at level 21.

Disadvantages:

  • Shared entropy: Divine spells have a 5% chance to trigger a wild surge.
  • Alignment is restricted to those allowed to Azuth's clergy.
Special: At character creation, the theurgical explorer automatically becomes a multiclass cleric/wild mage.
Notes for this kit

Create a Theurgical Explorer by selecting a human cleric. After the game begins, the character automatically becomes a cleric/mage multiclass and receives a few spells, some chosen at random, as if selected during mage creation.

Playing tips: contingencies and sequencers with cleric spells

Minor Sequencer

  • War God’s Favor: Bless + Chant.

Contingency

  • Second Wind: hit points below 25% → Heal.
  • Unstoppable: character unable to move → Free Action.
  • Backlash: character hit → Blade Barrier.

Spell Sequencer

  • Bodyguards from Beyond the Grave (minimum level 15): Animate Dead + Animate Dead + Animate Dead.
  • Venom’s Curse: Doom + Greater Malison + Poison.

Spell Trigger

  • Destructive Fury (cast Black Blade of Disaster beforehand): Tenser’s Transformation + Righteous Magic + Improved Haste.
  • Steel Scourge: Blade Barrier + Blade Barrier + Blade Barrier.
  • Pseudo-Rest: improved Wondrous Recall + improved Wondrous Recall + improved Wondrous Recall, restoring 12 spells.
  • Mass Revitalization: Mass Cure + Mass Cure + Mass Cure.
  • Fury of the Air: Aerial Servant + Aerial Servant + Aerial Servant.

Chain Contingency

  • Unerring Reckless Dweomer (oBG2/BGT only): Improved Chaos Shield + Improved Chaos Shield + Improved Chaos Shield. Although they normally do not stack, Chaos Shields cast simultaneously add together, making Nahal’s Reckless Dweomer unerring.
  • Elemental Army: Elemental Summoning + Elemental Summoning + Elemental Summoning.
  • Here Come the Lords of Evil: Gate + Gate + Gate.
  • Battalion of the Forsaken (oBG2/BGT only): Summon Fallen Deva + Summon Fallen Deva + Summon Fallen Deva. The same principle as Unerring Reckless Dweomer applies: summoning three devas simultaneously bypasses the limit.
Improved Beast MasterRanger kit enhancement

This ranger is a loner, rarely at ease in urban surroundings. Therefore, <PRO_HESHE> maintains a natural affinity for animals; they are <PRO_HISHER> friends and comrades-in-arms, and the Beast Master has a limited form of telepathic communication with them.

Advantages:

  • +15% to Move Silently and Hide in Shadows.

Note for this kit

A trick bordering on cheating

In original BG2, ranger spells are combined with cleric spells. Raise a Beast Master to level 12 (or 13), then dual-class to cleric: once the cleric levels exceed the ranger levels, the character can cast Spider Spawn as a level 1 spell, Conjure Carrion Crawlers as a level 2 spell, and so on.

This is also possible in the Enhanced Editions if you enable the option that combines ranger and cleric spells. The option is hidden by default; some mods expose it.
